• Proxy engine updated to meow-rs 0.21.2: BoringSSL is now the only TLS stack, Hysteria2 runs on quiche, and the tunnel extension is smaller
• Configs whose nameservers point at their own dns.listen port now resolve proxy servers correctly
• DNS prefers IPv4 with a per-family cache, reuses tcp:// upstream connections, and accepts rule-set: patterns in nameserver-policy
• Every outbound dial has a global timeout and failing group members are skipped faster
• GLOBAL mode uses the primary outbound
• Deleting a connection or reloading the profile cancels the live stream; UDP DNS honours routing rules
• AnyTLS, Snell and Hysteria2 reliability fixes; new XHTTP transport
