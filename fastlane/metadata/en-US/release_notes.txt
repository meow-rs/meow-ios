meow 2.0

• New: meow for Apple TV. Send a config from your iPhone through iCloud — swipe right on a config and tap "iCloud Drive" — and it appears on the TV
• New: Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center widgets turn the VPN on or off and switch between Rule, Global and Direct
• New: subscriptions can update themselves every day or every week
• iPad and iPhone Duo: sidebar tabs, two-column Proxy Groups and Engine layouts, fold-aware columns on the inner display, and readable-width forms on wide screens
• New: Settings › Diagnostics › Crash Reports collects crash and hang reports from iOS so you can share them with an issue
• The status icon on the Home tab now matches the app icon you picked
• Configs that list dhcp:// DNS servers now start instead of failing
• Proxy engine updated to meow-rs 0.21.2: BoringSSL is now the only TLS stack, Hysteria2 runs on quiche, and the tunnel extension is smaller
• Configs whose nameservers point at their own dns.listen port now resolve proxy servers correctly
• DNS prefers IPv4 with a per-family cache, reuses tcp:// upstream connections, and accepts rule-set: patterns in nameserver-policy
• Every outbound dial has a global timeout and failing group members are skipped faster
• GLOBAL mode uses the primary outbound
• Deleting a connection or reloading the profile cancels the live stream; UDP DNS honours routing rules
• AnyTLS, Snell and Hysteria2 reliability fixes; new XHTTP transport
• Requires iOS 18 or later

Issues and feedback: https://github.com/meow-rs/meow-ios/issues
